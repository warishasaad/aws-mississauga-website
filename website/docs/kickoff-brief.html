MVP

Launch in three steps

The MVP is the smallest version that lets people find us, sign up for events, and volunteer or offer a talk, with their details handled safely.

Step 1 · Soft launch

Live with real content

  • Site live on our own domain with HTTPS
  • Real organizers, next 2 to 3 events, real Meetup and LinkedIn links
  • Our Code of Conduct and a contact address
  • Forms hidden or marked "coming soon"

Goal: share the link at the next meetup.

Step 2 · MVP

Forms that work, safely

  • Volunteer, speaker and sponsor forms stored securely in Canada
  • Organizers alerted by email, without applicant details in the email
  • Admin portal: organizer sign-in with MFA to review submissions and add or edit organizers, events, speakers and sponsors
  • Privacy notice on every form, spam protection

Goal: a volunteer form reaches only the right reviewer.

Step 3 · After MVP

Grow the community features

  • Newsletter with confirmed sign-up
  • Past-event slides and recordings
  • Member portal and resource library
  • QR check-in at meetups

Goal: members come back to the site between meetups.

Not in the MVP: member accounts, check-in and attendance exports. They're valuable, but they're not needed to launch.

Direction

How we'll make decisions

Community first

Every feature should help someone attend, learn, speak, volunteer or find work. If it doesn't, it waits.

The site is a learning project

Built on AWS serverless services, in code, so volunteers gain real experience by building it.

Privacy by design

Collect only what we need, keep it in Canada, never publish contact details, and delete data on schedule.

Knowledge sharing, not sales

Sponsors and speakers share what they've built. Nobody sells from the stage or the site.

Every level is welcome

Content is tagged 100, 200 and 300 so beginners and experts both find their place.

Ship small, ship often

Small pull requests, reviewed by a second person, deployed automatically.

Decisions needed at the kickoff

  1. What's our domain name?
  2. Who owns the AWS account and pays the bill?Our cost estimate is roughly $10 to $15 a month
  3. What's the soft launch date and the MVP date?
  4. Who are the first organizer accounts, and which forms does each person review?
  5. How long do we keep form submissions?Suggestion: 12 months after they're closed
  6. Do we write our own Code of Conduct, or adopt an existing one?
  7. Newsletter through AWS email, or an existing mailing tool?
